Documented Calculation Methodology

• People Served Calculation: Standardized food pantry metric where 1 collected portion/unit corresponds to approximately 2.5 nutritional meals served across families and community soup kitchens.

• Environmental Waste Model: Rescued food units are calculated using an empirical average of 0.75 kg per unit. Each 1.0 kg of food diverted from municipal landfills prevents 2.5 kg of atmospheric methane and carbon dioxide equivalent emissions.

• Live Synchronization: Data points are dynamically aggregated on-demand from JPA repositories and MySQL tables (food_donations and food_requests).
